# Privacy Policy

Tottoro does not collect any data.

- Your accounts and their secret keys are stored in your iCloud Keychain. Apple encrypts it end to end, and the developer of Tottoro cannot read it.
- Tottoro has no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not connect to the internet itself.
- Service logos are built into the app. Tottoro does not download them, so nobody learns which services you use.
- Your settings, such as the lock delay, are stored on your device only.
- When you export your accounts, they go to the app you choose, through the system. When you import, Tottoro reads only the file you choose.

If you have questions, open a discussion at https://github.com/TaiyoYamada/tottoro/discussions.
